2. 최종 프로젝트: Motion Planning-Bootstrapped RL
================================================================================

2.1 제목 및 핵심 아이디어
-----------------------
**Title:**
"Motion Planning-Bootstrapped Reinforcement Learning with Contact-Aware
Reward Shaping for High-DOF Dexterous Manipulation"

**핵심 아이디어:**
1. MoveIt2 motion planner로 자동 demonstration 생성 (1000 demos per robot)
2. Behavior Cloning으로 policy 초기화 (50 epochs)
3. Contact-aware rewards와 함께 PPO fine-tuning (5000 epochs)
4. High-DOF dexterous hands (26-DOF, 12-DOF)에 적용

**Three-Phase Framework:**

Phase 1: Automated Demonstration Generation (Isaac Sim + MoveIt2)
- MoveIt2로 collision-free trajectories 계획
- Isaac Sim에서 실행하여 (state, action) pairs 기록
- 로봇당 1000 demonstrations 생성
- Isaac Lab format으로 변환

Phase 2: Behavior Cloning Pre-training
- 3-layer FC network (256-256-128 units)
- 50 epochs, MSE loss
- 목표: >30% success rate

Phase 3: RL Fine-tuning + Contact-Aware Rewards
- BC policy로 초기화
- PPO training with contact-specific rewards (5000 epochs)
- Demonstration buffer 유지: 25% demo + 75% RL data

2.2 핵심 기여: Contact-Aware Reward Shaping
---------------------------------------
세 가지 contact-specific rewards:

(1) **Contact Gating**: Position tracking reward를 finger-object contact 시에만 활성화
    → 팔로만 밀지 않고 손가락으로 잡도록 강제

(2) **Grasp Duration**: 다중 손가락의 지속적 접촉 장려 (최소 10 steps)
    → 순간적 터치가 아닌 안정적 파지 유도

(3) **Ground Penalty**: Object-floor collision에 벌점
    → 바닥 튕김 악용 방지

**Key Insight:**
Motion planning은 kinematic feasibility를 제공하고,
Contact rewards는 dynamic stability를 제공하여,
상호 보완적으로 robust grasping 가능하게 함.

2.3 실험 설계
-----------
**Ablation Study (4 variants):**
1. Pure PPO (baseline)
2. BC + PPO
3. PPO + Contact
4. BC + PPO + Contact (full method)

**실험 구성:**
- 4 variants × 2 robots = 8 experiments
- 각 실험당 5000 epochs
- GPU parallelization (4096 envs)

**평가 지표:**
- Success rate
- Sample efficiency (samples needed to reach 80% success)
- Contact force statistics
- Grasp quality

**예상 결과:**
- Full method: 2-4× faster convergence vs pure PPO
- 80%+ success rate
- 50-75% training time reduction

3.1 주요 관련 논문 (검증 완료)
----------------------------

**[핵심] Abbatematteo et al. 2021 - Bootstrapping Motor Skill Learning**
- Publication: IROS 2021, arXiv:2101.04736
- Link: http://irl.cs.brown.edu/pubs/bootstrapping_skills.pdf
- 핵심:
  * Kinematic motion planning으로 demonstration 자동 생성
  * Behavior cloning + Policy gradient
  * Tasks: Drawer, microwave, t-ball
  * Robot: 7-DOF arm만 (simple gripper 또는 gripper 없음)
- 한계:
  ❌ Dexterous hand 없음 (6-7 DOF arm만)
  ❌ Contact dynamics 무시
  ❌ Simple manipulation tasks

**[최신] PLANRL 2024 - Motion Planning and Imitation Learning**
- Publication: arXiv:2408.04054, 2024
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.04054
- Authors: Amisha Bhaskar, Zahiruddin Mahammad, Sachin Jadhav, Pratap Tokekar
- 핵심:
  * Mode switching: Classical planning (waypoint) + RL (manipulation)
  * 3-network architecture: ModeNet, NavNet, InteractNet
  * Real robot: UR3e + Robotiq Hand-e gripper (parallel-jaw)
  * 90% success on simple tasks, unique success on two-stage tasks
  * Demonstration: Manual teleoperation (modified Gello, 10-20 demos)
- 한계:
  ❌ Simple parallel-jaw gripper만 (~8 DOF total)
  ❌ 수동 teleoperation 필요 (자동화 안 됨)
  ❌ Contact-aware rewards 없음
  ❌ High-DOF dexterous hand 없음

**[참고] DexPBT 2023**
- Publication: RSS 2023
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.12127
- 핵심:
  * Kuka (7-DOF) + Allegro (16-DOF) = 23-DOF
  * Population-based training
  * Isaac Gym GPU parallelization
- 차이점:
  * Demonstration 사용 안 함 (pure RL)
  * Motion planning bootstrap 없음

3.2 우리 연구의 차별점 (Novelty)
------------------------------
**기존 연구의 공통 한계:**
- Abbatematteo 2021: 6-7 DOF arm만, contact 무시
- PLANRL 2024: Simple gripper, 수동 teleoperation, contact 무시
- DexPBT 2023: Pure RL (샘플 비효율), demonstration 없음

**우리의 3가지 핵심 기여:**

✅ **(1) Scaling to High-DOF Systems**
   - 기존 7-DOF → 우리 26-DOF dexterous hand
   - 3-4× larger action space
   - Motion planning bootstrap가 high-DOF에서도 효과적임을 검증

✅ **(2) Kinematic-Dynamic Complementarity**
   - 기존: Kinematic planning만 (contact dynamics 무시)
   - 우리: Motion planning (kinematic) + Contact rewards (dynamic)
   - 상호 보완적 접근으로 robust grasping 달성

✅ **(3) Automated Demonstration Pipeline**
   - PLANRL: Manual teleoperation (Gello 하드웨어, 10-20 demos)
   - 우리: Fully automated (MoveIt2, 1000 demos)
   - 인간 개입 없이 대규모 시연 생성 (\$0 hardware cost)

**Academic Positioning:**
"This research extends motion planning-bootstrapped RL [Abbatematteo 2021]
from 7-DOF robotic arms to 26-DOF dexterous hands, validating sample
efficiency gains in high dimensions and introducing contact-aware reward
shaping to address the kinematic-dynamic gap that motion planning alone
cannot capture."

-----------------------

| **시연 생성** | 수동 teleoperation (Gello) | 자동 (MoveIt2) |

| **아키텍처** | 3-network (Mode/Nav/Interact) | Single policy + contact rewards |
| **기여** | Mode switching | High-DOF + Automated + Contact |

    'rewards': [...],         # reward signal
